# Example prompts

Plain requests that work well once wptaskify is connected. More at https://wptaskify.com/prompts.

## WordPress
- "Write and publish a 1,500-word post about choosing a water softener, with headings, an FAQ and internal links."
- "Find posts missing a meta description and write one for each."
- "Fix missing alt text on my site."
- "Which of my sites lost traffic last week?" (needs Search Console connected)

## WooCommerce
- "Add this product with a description and SEO title, price 29 and stock 40."
- "Show me today's orders and mark order 1042 as completed."

## Shopify
- "Write SEO titles and meta descriptions for every product that is missing them."
- "Create a 15% discount code for the summer collection."

## Pinterest
- "Make 5 pins from my 3 best posts and schedule them across this week."
- "Which pins got the most saves last month?"

## Safety
- "Delete the draft posts older than a year." (asks for your approval first)
